The reality is that sales and marketing stacks process vast amounts of personal data—names, emails, phone numbers, behavioral tracking, and even sensitive financial or demographic details. Without rigorous privacy testing and monitoring, organizations can unknowingly expose customer data to security vulnerabilities, compliance violations, and reputational harm.
